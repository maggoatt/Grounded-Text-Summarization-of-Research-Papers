Focal loss is not a proper scoring rule, and training with it can yield underconfident or overconfident estimators. We prove that minimizing FCL yields superior probability and classification calibration compared to Focal loss alone. We evaluate the effectiveness of our proposed method on both image and text classification tasks. Focal loss sharpens the model's focus on challenging examples. Calibration of deep neural networks (DNNs) is critical for reliable uncertainty estimation. Focal loss provides the most well-defined and precise activation areas. FCL not only enhances model calibration but also refines anomaly localization. Minimizing the Focal Calibration Loss L  FCL mitigates overconfidence (OC) and underconfidence (UC) in the predicted probabilities of a classifier. The ECE can be approximated by grouping predictions into M bins and calculating the average confidence and accuracy for each bin. Focal calibration loss (FCL) improves interpretability and performance in medical imaging tasks.
